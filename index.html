01 / THE APPLICATIONS

Four different jobs. One research discipline.

The same evidence-first machinery can answer very different questions. Choose the problem you’re trying to solve.

01 · B2B BUYER INTELLIGENCE OUTREACH

Find the people worth talking to.

Fewer names. Better reasons to make contact.

Boswell qualifies prospects before writing a word. It checks who they are, researches their public record, develops sourced interpretations of their priorities and communication style, and drafts outreach in the sender’s voice. It also tells you whom not to pursue.

THE OUTPUT
Qualified worklist · verified dossiers · individually tailored messages

02 · EXPERT KNOWLEDGE AI PERSONAS

Build expert judgment into AI.

Not a costume. A traceable body of knowledge.

Boswell studies an expert’s published work, talks, articles, and documented positions, then constructs a versioned research collection: principles, disagreements, methods, and the evidence behind them. Those collections can inform specialized AI personas without pretending the source expert authored the AI’s decisions.

THE OUTPUT
Canonical research collections · sourced principles · persona knowledge releases
03 · B2C AUDIENCE INTELLIGENCE CUSTOMERS

Learn more from customers you already have.

Beyond demographics and the last purchase.

The B2C application is intended to examine appropriately governed customer records, language, and behavioral evidence for recurring motivations, objections, and decision patterns. The goal is better audience hypotheses and more useful targeting, not a diagnosis or a claim to know what is in someone’s head.

THE OUTPUT
Mindset hypotheses · audience patterns · research and targeting priorities

Walk into the room prepared.

For the conversation you can’t afford to waste.

02 / THE METHOD

More research isn’t enough. The research has to survive scrutiny.

Boswell is built around a simple rule: no unsupported finding should quietly become a confident statement. The engine separates source material, interpretation, criticism, and the action that follows.

01 / COLLECT
Search by meaning.
Gather relevant public evidence and authorized client-provided material. Preserve the original record.
02 / VERIFY
Check whose words they are.
Resolve identities, authorship, source quality, and conflicts before drawing conclusions.
03 / CHALLENGE
Test the interpretation.
Independent critiques expose weak inferences, missing evidence, and overstated certainty.
04 / APPLY
Produce useful work.
Deliver an outreach plan, expert knowledge collection, audience hypothesis, or conversation brief.
READ WITH THE SOURCES ATTACHED

Language can support hypotheses about a person’s expressed priorities and communication habits. It cannot establish hidden motives, clinical traits, or future behavior with certainty. Boswell’s outputs must be read with their sources and limitations attached.

03 / THE EXPERIMENT

Same AI writer. Same prospect. Different intelligence.

For the B2B outreach application, I tested the same writing model with and without Boswell’s research and decision process. The engine’s version was preferred in all six blind assessments across three executives by two independent AI judges.

The useful finding wasn’t just the result. When we deliberately gave the engine the wrong communication approach, its advantage disappeared. Understanding how to speak to a person mattered more than simply adding detail.

04 / THE BUILDER

I built Boswell because the interesting information is usually already there.

People leave a remarkable record in what they write, publish, explain, defend, and refuse. Most systems skim it for keywords and titles. Boswell was designed to read it much more closely, preserve what can be verified, and make that research useful.
